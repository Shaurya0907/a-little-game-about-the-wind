# A Little Game About The Wind 
> "A Little Game About The Wind" is a small indie project made by me which mainly requires fast reflexes to beat the game<br> 

### What makes this game so special (according to me)

1. The idea is literally very unique to me even.
2. I made it from scratch with blank idea in like 9 hours till like 30 min before the end
3. It is not an AI Slop. I dont like Ai slop games even if they can be made within a short span of time, No, it is not what we want

### About the game

You are a small guy who is lost in the forest. Your task is to return to your home alive. You have 3 lives and every damage takes each life at a time. "Wind" will act like a driving force which will stop you from going back and will damage you OFTEN. You must parry at the RIGHT time (its mentioned WHEN to parry in the game itself) or you will take DAMAGE.
Actually i wanted to add like 5 more obstacles but due to shortage of time i had to go with one only.
